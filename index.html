Good to know

confirm policy details before launch
How do online orders ship?

Singles ship sleeved in a top loader (we are who we say we are) inside a bubble mailer, with tracking. Sealed product ships boxed. Or skip shipping entirely — choose in-store pickup and it's waiting at the counter.

How do you grade card condition?

Every single in the case and on the site carries a condition label — NM (near mint) or LP (lightly played) — checked by hand before it's listed. If something arrives not as described, email toploadedtcg@gmail.com and we'll make it right.

Can you hold a card for me?

Usually, yes — call (513) 222-2573 during open hours and we can set an in-stock item aside for a short hold while you head over.

How does selling a collection work?

Walk in any day we're open — no appointment needed — or start a quote from the Sell to Us page. We sort it, price it against current market, and make an offer with zero obligation. Estate-sized lot? We travel across the Tri-State.

What's the store credit bonus?

Take any buylist or trade offer as store credit instead of cash and we add 10% on top. Credit is tracked on your account at the register — just give your name or phone number at the counter.

When and where is the card show?

The first Saturday or Sunday of every month, 10 AM to 4 PM at the Hilton Cincinnati Airport, 7373 Turfway Rd, Florence, KY. Admission is free; vendors set up from 8 AM.
